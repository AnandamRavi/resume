Case Study · Blended Learning Redesign

Training for Cloud Solution Architects

A 4-week, fully virtual intensive program — heavy on hands-on activity, but also heavy on material many attendees already knew, and heavy on facilitators' calendars. The brief: convert it to self-paced learning wherever possible, keep the synchronous time meaningful, and give facilitators their time back.

The requirement

The Cloud Solution Architect training programs were originally designed as 4-week intensive virtual sessions, with time split between exhaustive hands-on activities and material many attendees already knew or could easily pick up on their own. The all-virtual format also put a heavy strain on facilitators' time.

The ask: convert much of this into self-paced learning, keep the synchronous sessions valuable, and free up facilitator hours.

The design — the "Wow!" factor

What made this course different was the resourceful mixing of tools and platforms to optimize time, ease facilitation, and improve the learner experience.

We delivered learning through the Desire2Learn (D2L) blended learning platform, which offers strong reporting, scoring, tracking and gating — but relies heavily on HTML coding, which created two problems: developers proficient in HTML weren't on hand, and even well-coded D2L pages could look a little drab.

The fix: build the courses in Articulate Rise and Storyline — which called for design skill more than coding skill, which our team had in abundance — and embed those directly into D2L. Best of both platforms.

My role

As the ID manager facing a 25-hour curriculum requirement on D2L — without the in-house coding talent that platform usually demands — I proposed building the entire course in Articulate and embedding it into D2L, then trained my team on the approach. I also project-managed the full course, setting expectations and educating clients on the platforms and tools involved.

The detailed design

The course moved from a 100% virtual format to blended learning hosted on Desire2Learn, with less than 40% of learning hours spent in synchronous sessions. It covered 4 topics, each delivered across 3 formats.

D2L enabled a gated learning experience — starting with a readiness test to check pre-requisite knowledge, plus checks that ensured learners completed self-paced material before joining cohort sessions. The D2L interface opened with interactive, animated clickable modules built in Articulate Storyline and embedded directly into the platform.

The impact

50%Cut in total time
25hTotal program length
9hFaculty time required

Learners were never required to spend more than 2 hours in VILT or cohort-based sessions. Here's how the redesign addressed the original program's challenges:

Challenges and how they were mitigated
Category Original virtual program Redesigned blended learning
Faculty availability Facilitators needed to be available 50+ hours across the 4-week period. Program cut to 9 days (25 hours); facilitator availability required for just 9 hours — an 82% saving.
Session attendance Attendance dropped sharply in weeks 3 & 4 as participants struggled to keep taking time away from work. Participants completed all 3 components within the 9-day program.
Consistency in experience No consistency in content or presentation — each facilitator updated slides and ran sessions their own way. All material delivered via D2L and authored in Articulate, keeping content consistently engaging and relevant.
Tracking & scoring Difficult to track and score progress across multiple sessions and submissions. D2L's tracking, reporting and scoring functionality gave accurate, reliable completion reports.
