Post-Covid Onboarding for New Joiners

One of the many casualties of the pandemic was the loss of engagement and community — especially among our youngest, newest population, who hadn't even had a chance to meet each other or step inside an actual office. For this audience, we built a 7-week blended learning onboarding program using crosswords, word searches, and other gamified techniques, worked through in pairs and groups.

The requirement

With a holistic focus on skill development, collaboration, support, and getting comfortable with the Accenture way of working, the program aimed to equip new analysts with the foundational skills they needed to start building a career — and a strong sense of community and belonging.

The objectives:

  • Improve time to competency using a virtual blended training model
  • Improve the new-joiner experience by giving analysts opportunities to network and interact with leaders
  • Improve new-joiner engagement and reduce attrition

All the word games were built in Excel using features like conditional formatting to make them functional. Teams hosted virtual sessions and brown bags. Desire2Learn hosted the quizzes and topics, chosen for its reporting, tracking, and front-end experience.

My role

I managed a small team of 4 designers and developers to conceive, build, and deliver this 7-week program in a record 8 weeks. Starting from a completely blank slate, I spoke with stakeholders to understand the need and shape the program — and personally developed all 24 Excel-based, game-based quiz questions, which the developer then embedded into Desire2Learn.

Detailed design

Each track opened with a gamified quiz: 8 questions, 25 points each, with 150 of 200 points needed to pass. Every question was a puzzle — and at the start of each one, learners got a link to the matching self-paced topic they could use to find the answer.

The goal was to decipher a secret sentence: each correctly answered question revealed one word. Get every question right, and the full sentence unlocked.

Think of it like a city. For each track — each "city" — the quiz was Main Street: learners entered through it, which kept them from getting lost. The topics were Side Streets: to answer a question, learners were sent off to explore a specific topic, then returned to the quiz to complete it and move to the next question, and the next topic. Topics weren't compulsory, since learners arrived with different levels of prior knowledge — the more they already knew, the shorter their walk down Main Street.

Program impact

The group this program was built for made real strides toward its talent pipeline — upskilling, retaining, and engaging analysts for future growth. Based on supervisor surveys and performance data:

  • Analysts knew better what was expected of them in their roles; their confidence handling daily tasks and challenges improved, along with comfort using Word, Excel, and PowerPoint
  • Analysts felt better equipped to navigate their career journey at Accenture
  • Supervisors noted improved performance among analysts who attended the program
