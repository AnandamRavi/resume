Training to Transition to a New Cloud Platform

My team and I worked on a series of trainings on one of Accenture's Cloud-based platforms, intended for employees in sales, advisory, and solution-architecting roles. Leadership wanted the target audience comfortable with the platform fast — and a complex new platform is always a little intimidating, so the program nudged learners step-by-step into getting their feet wet before the plunge.

The requirement

  • Inform managers and above in Cloud Sales and Solutioning of the capabilities in the new platform
  • Break through the barriers of using a new platform
  • Make the best use of Managers' and Senior Managers' time

The design

  • Used multiple tools and platforms in conjunction with each other
  • Used pre-assessments and practice scenarios with game elements
  • Made the core learning optional depending on learners' pre-existing knowledge and skills, reducing unnecessary seat time

My role

As Senior Instructional Design Lead, I managed a team of about 5 designers — bringing my knowledge of tools to the fore to help design a workable solution, reviewing my team's work, and partnering with Subject Matter Experts and stakeholders.

Tools & platforms

Articulate Rise Articulate Storyline WordPress SharePoint myLearning (Accenture LMS)

Detailed design

1

Warm Up

Learners started their journey from a WordPress page, which directed them to other platforms — beginning with a pre-assessment quiz in a game-show format, built in Articulate Storyline and hosted on Accenture's LMS, myLearning. It included scoring, tracking, and recommended topics for learners to focus on.

2

Learn

Video-intensive learning topics were built in Articulate Rise and hosted on the SharePoint site — untracked and completely optional.

3

Practice

A Jeopardy-style quiz built in Articulate Storyline, including a self-scoring long-text question that measured similarity using the Sørensen–Dice coefficient, built into a bit of JavaScript. Note: I'm not a JavaScript coder — I tweaked and reused code snippets from the generous Articulate community.

4

Play

An open-ended form, built in SharePoint, accessible only once every other component was successfully completed.

90%

Attendance rate, with strong positive feedback from both participants and stakeholders.
